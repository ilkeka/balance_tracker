Balance Tracker is a simple, private income and expense tracker. Write down what you spend and what you earn, and see at a glance where your money actually goes.

Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud, no advertising and no analytics - the app does not even ask for the internet permission.

What you can do

- Add income and expenses with amount, date, category and a description.
- Sort your entries into categories such as bills, groceries, shopping, transport, travel, salary or reimbursement.
- See your current balance, total income and total expenses for the month on the start screen.
- Follow how your spending develops over time with a chart, and browse your latest transactions.
- Open the details of any transaction later on, and edit or delete it.

Sync without a server

You can copy your transactions to another device of your own over Bluetooth Low Energy, straight from the Local sync screen: pick the device next to you, confirm a pairing code on both sides, and the app merges what is missing. Nothing is uploaded anywhere and no account is needed.
